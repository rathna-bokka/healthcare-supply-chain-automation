# Business Impact

## Business Problem

Healthcare supply-chain teams often receive data from multiple ERP exports and manually review large spreadsheets to identify stock-outs, expiry exposure, blocked inventory, and delivery-performance issues. This approach can be slow, repetitive, and vulnerable to missed exceptions.

## Solution Impact

This project demonstrates how automation and business intelligence can improve the supply-risk monitoring process.

### Reduced Manual Work

The file-processing workflow automatically validates, copies, and logs ERP files. This reduces repetitive checking and creates a consistent process for preparing data.

### Faster Risk Identification

The risk-detection workflow evaluates monthly inventory records and immediately identifies products approaching expiry while stock remains available.

### Timely Notifications

Automated Outlook alerts provide product, market, inventory, and expiry information so supply-chain teams can act without waiting for a manual report.

### Duplicate-Alert Control

The alert log prevents repeated emails for the same product, market, month, and risk type. This reduces notification fatigue and keeps alerts relevant.

### Improved Management Visibility

The Power BI dashboards provide consolidated monitoring of:

* OTIF performance
* Service level
* Forecast accuracy
* Inventory availability
* Stock-outs
* Blocked inventory
* Expiry exposure
* SLOB risk

### Better Decision Support

The solution helps users identify products requiring attention and supports decisions such as inventory transfers, replenishment prioritization, blocked-stock review, and expiry-risk mitigation.

## Potential Operational Benefits

In a production environment, this solution could help:

* Shorten the time required to review monthly supply data
* Reduce the risk of overlooked stock and expiry exceptions
* Improve consistency in ERP file processing
* Support faster escalation of supply risks
* Strengthen inventory and service-level monitoring
* Provide traceable processing and alert records

## Project Scope

This is a simulated portfolio project created with synthetic pharmaceutical supply-chain data. The solution demonstrates the workflow and technical approach but does not claim measured savings from a live business implementation.
